About Us

Saving Lives Through
Community & Technology

UAP-BloodConnect is an inter-university blood donation platform connecting UAP students, faculty, and staff to ensure no life is lost to blood shortage within our community.

{% if not user.is_authenticated %} Join the Community {% endif %} Become a Donor

Our Mission

To create a reliable, efficient, and community-driven platform that bridges the gap between blood donors and recipients within the University of Asia Pacific community. We believe no one should suffer due to blood shortages when willing donors are nearby.

Our Vision

To eliminate preventable deaths due to blood shortages by leveraging technology and community participation — making blood donation accessible, efficient, and rewarding for every member of the UAP community and beyond.

Our Advantages

Why UAP-BloodConnect?

Built specifically for the UAP community with features inspired by the world's best blood donation platforms.

Emergency Response
Instant connection during critical situations when every second counts. Donors are alerted immediately when a matching blood type is needed.
Verified Community
Exclusive to UAP students, faculty and staff — verified with UAP IDs ensuring trust, safety, and genuine community connections.
Easy to Use
Simple, intuitive interface designed for quick access during emergencies. Find a matching donor in seconds with smart filtering.
Safety First
90-day donation interval enforcement, verified UAP members only, secure communication, and privacy protection built-in.
Achievement System
Inspired by global platforms like Red Cross and OneBlood — earn badges and recognition for your life-saving contributions.
Smart Notifications
Real-time notifications when your blood type is needed. Never miss a chance to save a life with instant alerts and response tracking.
