Process

How It Works

Four simple steps to connect donors with recipients in minutes.

1
Register
Sign up with your UAP ID. Verify your identity and select your blood type and location.
2
Request
Post a blood request with urgency level, blood group, hospital, and deadline.
3
Match
Compatible donors get instant notifications. They respond with availability in real time.
4
Save Lives
Donor goes to the hospital, donates, logs it, and earns achievement badges. A life is saved.
Platform Features

Everything You Need to Save Lives

Built with inspiration from NHS, Red Cross, and Canadian Blood Services.

Emergency Matching
Critical requests are instantly matched with compatible available donors — sorted by blood type and location.
Achievement System
Earn Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Hero badges as you hit donation milestones. Show off your impact.
Verified Community
All members are verified UAP students and faculty — a closed, safe environment for blood donation.
Smart Notifications
Get notified the moment a blood request matching your type is posted. Never miss a chance to help.
Donation Tracking
Log every donation with dates, hospitals, and certificates. Get auto-notified when you're eligible again.
Live Leaderboard
Compete with fellow donors. The most lives saved earns the title of UAP Blood Hero.
