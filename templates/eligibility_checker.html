Donation Eligibility Criteria

Age: 17 – 65 years
First-time donors should be under 60. Regular donors may be up to 65.
Weight: Min 50 kg
A minimum body weight ensures safe blood volume extraction.
Good Health
No fever, cold, or ongoing illness on the day of donation.
90-Day Interval
At least 3 months between whole blood donations.
No Blood Thinners
Warfarin, aspirin, and similar medications can disqualify you.
No Recent Major Surgery
Wait at least 6 months after major operations or serious illnesses.
